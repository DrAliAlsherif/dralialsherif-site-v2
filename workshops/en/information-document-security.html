Information & Document Security in Digital Environments

أمن المعلومات والوثائق في البيئات الرقمية

The workshop builds a practical understanding of information and document security in information institutions: information classification, access control, protecting the integrity of digital documents, privacy and compliance, and incident response — with practice aligned to standards such as ISO 27001 and ISO 15489 for records management.

Training areaArchives & Information Security
LevelIntermediate
Suggested durationTwo training days (12 hours)
Delivery languageArabic
PrerequisitesWorking in records management, archives, systems or digital libraries

1 Workshop objectives

  • Classify information and documents by sensitivity and set handling policies for them
  • Design access controls (identity, permissions, least privilege)
  • Protect the integrity of digital documents and prevent tampering (signatures, hashes, audit logs)
  • Apply privacy and personal data protection principles and regulatory compliance
  • Draw up backup, recovery and business continuity plans
  • Build a response plan for information security incidents and document leaks

2 What the workshop covers

  • Information security concepts: confidentiality, integrity, availability, applied to documents
  • Information classification, handling policies and labelling
  • Identity and access management: authentication, authorisation, permission review
  • Protecting digital documents: encryption, digital signatures, timestamps, audit logs
  • Privacy and compliance: data protection principles, retention, secure disposal
  • Common threats: phishing, malware, social engineering, the insider threat
  • Backup, recovery and business continuity (BCP/DRP)
  • Incident response: detection, containment, investigation, lessons learned

3 Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Set out an information classification scheme and handling policy for their institution
  • Design a role-based access control matrix
  • Produce a documented backup and recovery plan
  • Build a simplified response playbook for document-leak incidents

4 Who it is for

  • Records, archives and document management specialists and managers
  • Systems and digital library officers
  • Compliance and data protection officers
  • IT teams responsible for protecting institutional content
