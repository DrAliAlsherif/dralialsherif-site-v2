Planning & Building Digital Repositories Based on Global Standards

تخطيط وبناء المستودعات الرقمية وفق المعايير العالمية

A comprehensive workshop covering the whole life cycle of building an institutional digital repository: from the needs study and policy setting, through platform selection (DSpace, Fedora and others), metadata architecture and workflow design, to operation, long-term digital preservation and impact measurement.

Training areaDigital Repositories
LevelIntermediate to advanced
Suggested durationThree training days (15 hours)
Delivery languageArabic
PrerequisitesBasic knowledge of metadata and library systems; prior reading on open access is helpful

1 Workshop objectives

  • Draft the repository's vision, objectives and policies (deposit, content, rights, privacy)
  • Compare open-source repository platforms and choose the one that best fits the institution
  • Design a metadata schema compatible with Dublin Core and MODS, and OAI-PMH interoperability policy
  • Build deposit, review and publication workflows and assign persistent identifiers (DOI/Handle)
  • Apply the OAIS model and the principles of long-term digital preservation
  • Set repository performance indicators (availability, growth, usage, citation)

2 What the workshop covers

  • Digital repository concepts, their types, and their relationship to open access and open science
  • The needs study, stakeholder analysis and building the business case
  • Governing policies: self and mediated deposit, copyright, embargo periods
  • System architecture: platforms, hosting, integration with university systems
  • Metadata: schemas, mandatory fields, controlled vocabularies, interoperability
  • Workflow: deposit forms, review steps, quality assurance, persistent identifiers
  • Digital preservation: OAIS, file formats, backup, contingency plans
  • Launch, promotion, operational governance and measurement indicators

3 Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Produce a complete policy document for an institutional digital repository
  • Design a workable metadata schema and deposit workflow
  • Draw up a reasoned comparison matrix for choosing the repository platform
  • Build a long-term digital preservation plan and a performance dashboard

4 Who it is for

  • Digital repository specialists in academic and research libraries
  • Directors of university libraries and research centres
  • Metadata, systems and research support specialists
  • Officers responsible for open access and institutional publishing initiatives
