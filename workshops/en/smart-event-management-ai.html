Smart Digital Transformation in Academic Event Management Using AI Tools

التحول الرقمي الذكي في إدارة الفعاليات الأكاديمية باستخدام أدوات الذكاء الاصطناعي

The workshop offers a practical framework for putting generative AI tools to work across the whole life cycle of an academic event — from the first idea for a conference or seminar through to the closing impact report — cutting delivery time, raising the quality of the output and automating repetitive work for organising teams in universities and information institutions.

Training areaArtificial Intelligence
LevelIntermediate
Suggested durationTwo training days (10 hours) — can be condensed into a single day
Delivery languageArabic
PrerequisitesBasic computer and internet skills, and hands-on experience organising at least one event or conference

1 Workshop objectives

  • Map the AI tools that suit each stage of event management
  • Build a complete event plan (scope, timeline, indicative budget) with help from generative models
  • Automate invitations and correspondence, and draft multilingual promotional copy
  • Use AI to screen abstracts, schedule sessions and assign reviewers
  • Design live dashboards for registration, attendance and engagement
  • Produce the closing report, analyse feedback and measure event impact automatically

2 What the workshop covers

  • The academic event life cycle and where AI enters at each stage
  • Prompt engineering for planning tasks: the event brief, the timeline, risk analysis
  • Automating marketing and correspondence: content generation, translation, channel scheduling
  • Managing scientific papers: abstract screening, similarity detection, matching papers to reviewers
  • Registration and participant service: chatbots, FAQs, live support
  • Dashboards and decision making during the event using live data
  • After the event: session transcription, minutes generation, impact reports and recommendations
  • Governance, ethics of use and protection of participant data

3 Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Design a delivery plan for an academic event with a specific AI tool assigned to each task
  • Build a reusable prompt library for organising teams
  • Create a dashboard tracking registration, attendance and engagement
  • Prepare an automated closing-report template linking objectives to results and impact
  • Draw up a governance and data-protection checklist for using these tools

4 Who it is for

  • Event and conference coordinators and managers in universities and information centres
  • Staff in corporate communication and public relations departments of libraries
  • Members of scientific and organising committees for conferences
  • Librarians tasked with organising seminars and workshops
