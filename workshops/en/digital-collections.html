Building & Developing Digital Collections in Libraries

بناء وتطوير المجموعات الرقمية في المكتبات

The workshop covers planning, building, growing, organising and delivering digital collections: from the digital collection development policy and content selection through digitisation or digital acquisition, description, rights, access, usage measurement and periodic review.

Training areaRepositories & Digital Collections
LevelIntermediate
Suggested durationTwo training days (12 hours)
Delivery languageArabic
PrerequisitesGeneral knowledge of collection development and metadata

1 Workshop objectives

  • Draft a development policy for digital collections with selection and withdrawal criteria
  • Identify sources of digital content (digitisation, deposit, purchase, open access, donation)
  • Design an organisational structure and descriptive, administrative and structural metadata for the collection
  • Handle copyright, licensing and access questions
  • Build access and discovery interfaces and connect them to the catalogue and repository
  • Measure collection usage and evaluate and review it periodically

2 What the workshop covers

  • What a digital collection is, its types, and how it relates to the repository and the archive
  • Digital collection development policy: scope, priorities, quality criteria
  • Content sources: digitisation projects, deposit, acquisition, partnerships
  • Metadata for digital collections: schemas, levels of description, mandatory fields
  • Rights: intellectual property, Creative Commons licences, rights statements
  • Access and discovery: platforms, interfaces, improving visibility, interlinking
  • Preservation, file management, formats and copies
  • Evaluation: usage indicators, user satisfaction, collection review

3 Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Produce a draft development policy for a specific digital collection
  • Design a metadata description template for the collection and a plan for organising it
  • Build a rights and licensing matrix for the collection's items
  • Draw up an access, usage measurement and periodic evaluation plan

4 Who it is for

  • Collection development and digital acquisitions specialists
  • Digitisation and digital library specialists
  • Curators of special and heritage collections
  • Digital content officers in libraries and information centres
