Automated Cataloging Using MARC 21: Advanced Applications

الفهرسة الآلية باستخدام مارك 21: تطبيقات متقدمة

An advanced workshop that goes beyond MARC 21 basics into complex applications: multi-level records, linking between records, batch editing, format conversion, and integration with semantic web data — with exercises on difficult cataloguing cases.

Training areaMetadata & Cataloguing
LevelAdvanced
Suggested durationThree training days (18 hours) — intensively hands-on
Delivery languageArabic
PrerequisitesCommand of MARC 21 basics and descriptive cataloguing, and real work on an integrated library system

1 Workshop objectives

  • Master the complex fields in MARC 21 (76X–78X linking fields, 880 alternate scripts, local fields)
  • Catalogue continuing and integrating resources, special materials and electronic resources
  • Carry out batch editing and conditional replacement using tools such as MarcEdit
  • Convert records between MARC 21, MARCXML, MODS and Dublin Core
  • Validate records automatically, and detect and fix errors
  • Connect MARC 21 to BIBFRAME and semantic web data as a route forward

2 What the workshop covers

  • A quick review of MARC 21 record structure, delimiters, indicators and subfield codes
  • Advanced fields: linking entries (76X–78X), analysis, alternate graphic representation (880)
  • Special cases: serials, series, collections, manuscripts, maps, media
  • Batch editing: regular expressions, tasks and templates in MarcEdit
  • Import, export, OAI harvesting, automated loading and duplicate detection
  • Conversion between formats and metadata schemas, and quality control afterwards
  • Validation: audit tools, error reports, correction plans
  • From MARC to BIBFRAME: concepts, differences, transition scenarios

3 Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Catalogue complex resources in MARC 21 accurately and consistently with policy
  • Carry out a batch editing project on a real set of records
  • Convert a set of records between two formats with a quality control report
  • Produce a checklist for automated record validation in their own system

4 Who it is for

  • Experienced cataloguers and metadata specialists
  • Staff responsible for record loading and bibliographic databases
  • Supervisors of cataloguing and technical services departments
  • Systems specialists involved in bibliographic data migration
