Digital Repositories, Archiving & Preservation in the Age of AI & Open Access

المستودعات الرقمية والأرشفة والحفظ في عصر الذكاء الاصطناعي والوصول الحر

A forward-looking workshop joining three threads — digital repositories, archiving, and long-term preservation — and rereading them in the light of two large shifts: the spread of generative AI, and the entrenchment of open-access and open-science policy, with application scenarios and governance.

Training areaDigital Repositories
LevelAdvanced
Suggested durationTwo training days (12 hours)
Delivery languageArabic
PrerequisitesPrior experience with digital repositories, digital archiving or research data management

1 Workshop objectives

  • Analyse the effect of open access and funder policy on the role of institutional repositories
  • Use AI to enrich metadata, control quality and improve discovery inside the repository
  • Fold research data and its management plans into the repository
  • Apply the FAIR principles, persistent identifiers and linking between outputs
  • Strengthen trustworthiness in the digital archive (CoreTrustSeal / OAIS)
  • Draft governance for AI use in the repository that preserves integrity and transparency

2 What the workshop covers

  • The shifting landscape: open access, funder mandates, open science, open data
  • AI inside the repository: descriptor generation, classification, duplicate detection, translation
  • Research data management: management plans, acquisition, description, data citation
  • The FAIR principles and persistent identifiers (DOI, ORCID, ROR) and linking networks
  • Long-term preservation, trustworthiness, and assessment and certification standards
  • Measuring impact: altmetrics, dashboards, compliance reporting
  • Risks: the quality of automated output, bias, copyright, machine-generated content
  • A roadmap for developing the repository over the next two years

3 Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Produce an assessment of their repository's position in the light of open-access policy
  • Design one AI application scenario for metadata enrichment, with its controls
  • Build a checklist for aligning the repository with the FAIR principles
  • Set out a two-year development roadmap for the repository

4 Who it is for

  • Managers and supervisors of institutional digital repositories
  • Research support and research data management specialists
  • Open access and publishing policy officers in universities
  • Digital archiving and long-term preservation specialists
