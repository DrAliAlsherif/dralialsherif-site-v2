Descriptive & Subject Cataloging According to RDA & LCSH

الفهرسة الوصفية والموضوعية وفق معياري RDA وLCSH

A hands-on workshop combining descriptive cataloguing under RDA and the IFLA-LRM model with subject cataloguing and authority control under the Library of Congress Subject Headings (LCSH), with exercises on varied material and the results mapped to MARC 21 fields.

Training areaMetadata & Cataloguing
LevelIntermediate
Suggested durationThree training days (18 hours)
Delivery languageArabic
PrerequisitesBasic knowledge of cataloguing and MARC 21; well suited to those moving from AACR2 to RDA

1 Workshop objectives

  • Understand the foundations of RDA, the IFLA-LRM model, and entity and relationship concepts
  • Record the core description elements and relationships under RDA in a MARC 21 environment
  • Build controlled access points for persons, corporate bodies and works
  • Analyse subject content and formulate main and subdivided LCSH headings
  • Use subdivisions (form, topical, geographic, chronological) and build heading strings
  • Keep descriptive and subject cataloguing consistent with library policy

2 What the workshop covers

  • From AACR2 to RDA: the philosophy, the structure, the working tools (RDA Toolkit)
  • The IFLA-LRM model: entities, attributes, relationships and their effect on description
  • RDA elements: title, responsibility, edition, production/publication, physical description, series
  • Controlled access points and relationships between entities (authors, works, expressions)
  • Subject analysis: determining content, subject policy, how many headings
  • LCSH: heading types, subdivisions, strings, search and verification tools
  • Mapping to MARC 21 fields (1XX, 6XX, 7XX, 8XX) and quality control
  • Comprehensive exercises on books, serials, electronic resources and audiovisual material

3 Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Create complete descriptive records under RDA for several material types
  • Formulate accurate, consistent LCSH headings with the right subdivisions
  • Build controlled access points and document relationships between entities
  • Produce a short policy guide for descriptive and subject cataloguing in their library

4 Who it is for

  • Cataloguers and descriptive and subject cataloguing specialists
  • Authority control and subject headings specialists
  • Librarians newly moving to RDA
  • Postgraduate students and trainers in knowledge organisation
