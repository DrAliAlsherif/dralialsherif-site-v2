Strategic Planning for Information Institutions in the Big-Data Era

التخطيط الاستراتيجي لمؤسسات المعلومات في عصر البيانات الضخمة

A leadership workshop connecting modern strategic planning tools to the reality of big data: how an information institution turns its data into a strategic advantage, builds a flexible plan grounded in indicators and foresight, and keeps pace with shifts in information behaviour and technology.

Training areaStrategic Planning
LevelAdvanced / leadership
Suggested durationTwo training days (12 hours) plus a follow-up session
Delivery languageArabic
PrerequisitesA supervisory or leadership post, and prior involvement in preparing operational plans

1 Workshop objectives

  • Analyse the internal and external environment with modern, data-backed tools
  • Draft the strategic direction (mission, vision, values, objectives) for an information institution
  • Build a strategy map and balanced performance indicators (BSC/OKR)
  • Fold big-data analytics and foresight into the planning cycle
  • Manage risk, scenarios and contingency plans
  • Align budget, resources and initiatives with strategic priorities

2 What the workshop covers

  • Contemporary strategic planning concepts and what is particular to information institutions
  • Big data: its sources in libraries, its governance, and turning it into decision insight
  • Analysis tools: PESTEL, the five forces, quantitative SWOT, stakeholder analysis
  • Foresight: trends, weak signals, scenario building
  • The strategy map, the indicator system and its link to initiatives
  • Delivery governance: follow-up, periodic review, performance management
  • Change management and strategic communication with users and partners
  • A hands-on session: a draft strategic plan for the participant's own institution

3 Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Produce a draft strategic framework for their institution (direction, objectives, indicators)
  • Build a strategy map and a balanced performance dashboard
  • Design two future scenarios and a response plan for each
  • Set out a mechanism for aligning the annual budget with strategic priorities

4 Who it is for

  • Directors of libraries and information centres, and their deputies
  • Planning, organisational development and quality officers
  • Leaders of digital transformation and knowledge management initiatives
  • Candidates for leadership posts in information institutions
