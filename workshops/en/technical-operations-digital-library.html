Managing Technical Operations in the Modern Digital Library

إدارة العمليات الفنية في بيئة المكتبة الرقمية الحديثة

The workshop redesigns the library's technical operations (acquisitions, cataloguing, authority control, serials, electronic resources) to suit a digital environment: a unified workflow, productivity indicators, automation of repetitive tasks, and system integration.

Training areaTechnical Operations
LevelIntermediate
Suggested durationThree training days (15 hours)
Delivery languageArabic
PrerequisitesWorking in — or supervising — acquisitions, cataloguing, authority control or serials

1 Workshop objectives

  • Map the current technical processes and identify bottlenecks and waste
  • Redesign the workflow on quality and efficiency (Lean) principles
  • Unify cataloguing and acquisitions policies and quality control
  • Automate repetitive tasks through system tools, light scripting and AI
  • Manage electronic resources, their life cycle (ERM) and their licences
  • Build productivity and quality indicators for technical operations, and periodic reporting

2 What the workshop covers

  • The technical operations system in a digital library and how its parts relate
  • Process maps, cycle-time analysis and points of delay
  • Modern acquisitions: purchasing models, demand-driven acquisition, subscriptions, PDA/DDA
  • Cataloguing in a digital environment: copy cataloguing, batches, automated loading
  • Authority control, quality control and automated record validation
  • Electronic resource management: licences, activation, COUNTER usage statistics
  • Automation: tools such as MarcEdit, scripting, and integration through system APIs
  • Productivity and quality dashboards, and a culture of continuous improvement

3 Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Draw an improved map of one technical process with an estimate of the savings expected
  • Produce a unified policy and procedures manual for their department
  • Apply at least one automation tool to a real recurring task
  • Build a monthly productivity and quality dashboard

4 Who it is for

  • Heads and members of acquisitions, cataloguing, serials and technical services departments
  • Authority control and quality assurance specialists
  • Electronic resources and library systems officers
  • Supervisors responsible for process development in libraries
