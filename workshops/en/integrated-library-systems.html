Using & Managing Integrated Library Systems (ILS)

استخدام وإدارة نظم المكتبات المتكاملة

An operational workshop covering the modules of an integrated library system (cataloguing, acquisitions, circulation, serials, the public catalogue) and its daily administration: permissions, policies, reports, maintenance and integration with other services — with examples from systems such as Koha, Symphony and Horizon.

Training areaLibrary Systems
LevelIntermediate
Suggested durationThree training days (15 hours)
Delivery languageArabic
PrerequisitesWorking in a library that uses an integrated system; system administration experience is not required

1 Workshop objectives

  • Grasp the architecture of an integrated library system and how its modules relate
  • Configure circulation policies, fines, and patron and material types
  • Manage permissions, staff accounts and audit logs
  • Run the acquisitions and serials modules, and track orders and budget
  • Extract operational and statistical reports and dashboards
  • Manage routine maintenance, backup and service continuity plans

2 What the workshop covers

  • The parts of an integrated library system and the shared database
  • System setup: patron types, materials, locations, the work calendar
  • The circulation module: rules, holds, renewals, fines, notices
  • The acquisitions and serials modules: purchase orders, vendors, budget, claiming
  • The public catalogue (OPAC/discovery): configuration, interfaces, the patron account
  • Reports: prebuilt, custom, direct queries, export
  • Security, permissions, the audit log and protection of patron data
  • Maintenance: updates, backup, the contingency plan, fault management

3 Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Configure a full set of circulation policies for one patron type and material type
  • Create a set of periodic operational reports for their department
  • Produce a permissions matrix for staff using the system
  • Draw up a maintenance, backup and service continuity plan

4 Who it is for

  • Library system operators in circulation, cataloguing, acquisitions and serials
  • New system librarians
  • Library supervisors responsible for daily operation
  • IT support teams working alongside the library
