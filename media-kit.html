2 Short bio (English)

  • Dr. Ali Fathy Alsherif is a Smart Systems & Digital Transformation Consultant, academic lecturer, researcher and international trainer in Library & Information Science, Digital Repositories, Archives, Knowledge Management and Artificial Intelligence, with more than twenty years of experience across leading academic and governmental institutions in the UAE and the Arab world. He holds a PhD in Library & Information Science, has authored three books, and is a Sharjah Library Literature Award laureate and UAE Golden Visa holder.
