# Projects — card image prompts

Artwork for the nine cards in the `#projects` section, to replace the SVG
diagrams they show now. Nine projects across four groups, and the art currently
repeats: `technical`, `metadata` and `library` each cover two cards.

## One rule that matters more here than anywhere else

**These cards name real institutions.** Jumeira University, 42 Abu Dhabi, the
Sheikh Saud Educational Foundation, the Government Empowerment Department, the
Hamad Bin Mohammed Center for Heritage Revival, the Ministry of Culture, the
National Library, the Tabah Foundation, the RTA.

So the image must never look like a photograph of that actual place. No
recognisable real building, no logo, no signage, no emblem, no flag, no
identifiable interior. Every prompt below is an abstract scene about *the work
that was done* — a repository founded, a system tagged, a collection
classified — not a depiction of the client. The prompts say so explicitly, and
an image that starts to look like a real, specific institution should be
rejected even if it is beautiful.

This matters because a visitor reading "National Library, UAE" beside a
photorealistic library interior will reasonably assume they are looking at that
library. They would not be.

## The register for this section

The other three grids each have their own:

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| Services | a **change** — scattered becoming ordered, stone becoming light |
| Areas of expertise | a **place** — a reading room, a vault, a hall of racks |
| Workshops | a **subject** — what the training is about |
| **Projects** | **a thing delivered** — something built, standing, finished |

Keep projects concrete and singular: one apparatus, one counter, one wall of
spines. Each scene below has been checked against the thirty-two images already
on the site so nothing repeats.

## Card geometry

| | |
|---|---|
| **Aspect** | 1600 × 660 px (`aspect-ratio: 16/6.6`) — the widest band on the site |
| **Overlapping elements** | none — all four corners free |
| **Save as** | `Project photos/<number>. <anything>.jpg` |

It is a **letterbox**. Compose wide and shallow: a tall subject will be cropped
top and bottom. Spread the scene horizontally.

## Reject an image if

* it has any writing in it — including call numbers, spine labels or signage;
* it resembles a real, identifiable institution, or carries any logo or emblem;
* the background is white or light grey;
* the subject is tall and narrow, and so survives the letterbox crop badly.

# The nine prompts

## 1 — Digital Repository for Jumeira University
**المستودع الرقمي لجامعة جميرا** · DSpace · 2020

*A repository founded and standing.*

> A single tall monolith of layered light standing complete on a dark plinth in
> an empty space, its horizontal strata glowing softly from within. Fine
> streams of small luminous fragments flow in low from both sides and disappear
> into its base. A faint aura of light rises from its top and disperses. Dark
> cinematic 3D illustration on a deep navy-black background, lit only by
> glowing cyan and violet light with soft volumetric haze. Wide shallow
> letterbox composition, subject centred, scene spread horizontally. An
> abstract concept image, not a real place — no recognisable building, no logo,
> no emblem, no signage. Absolutely no text anywhere in the image — no words,
> letters, numbers, captions or labels. Nothing written at all.

Put them in `Project photos/` and say so.

without one — so I will add the slugs in the table above before wiring the
photos. `tools/_photos.py` handles the crop, compression and `main.js` sync
already.

project without a photo keeps its drawing, and the sources stay out of git, as
with the other three sets.
