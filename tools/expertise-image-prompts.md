# Areas of expertise — card image prompts

Artwork for the ten cards in the `#expertise` section, to replace the SVG
diagrams they show now.

## The problem these prompts have to solve

Eight of the ten areas share a subject with a consulting service that already
has a photo on the site:

| Area | Service already illustrated |
|---|---|
| Digital Repositories | Digital Repository Development |
| Archives & Preservation | Archive Consulting |
| Artificial Intelligence | AI Consulting |
| Digital Transformation | Digital Transformation |
| Metadata Standards | Metadata Consulting |
| Research Support | Research Consulting |
| Training & Capacity | Training Programs |
| Consulting | (four of them) |

Describe the same idea twice and the two sections read as duplicates. So the
ten below are built on a deliberate split:

**A service image shows a change. An expertise image shows a place.**

The published service images are all transformations — scattered becoming
ordered, stone becoming light, one lamp lighting many. The expertise images are
worlds instead: a reading room, a vault, a hall of racks, a woven sphere. Same
subjects, different register, so the two grids never echo each other.

Each prompt below has also been checked against the fourteen workshop images
and the eight service images, so no scene repeats.

## Card geometry — the easiest of the three sections

| | |
|---|---|
| **Aspect** | 1600 × 840 px (`aspect-ratio: 16/8.4`) — 16:9 crops fine |
| **Overlapping elements** | none — no number, no icon badge |
| **Corners** | all four free; centre the subject normally |
| **Save as** | `Expertise photos/<number>. <anything>.jpg` |

## Still true, from the earlier sets

Describe a **scene, not a diagram** — every chart-shaped prompt in the first
workshop batch came back with burnt-in English headings. Reject any image with
writing in it, a white background, or subject matter that has drifted out of
the library and information world.

## 1 — Academic Libraries
**المكتبات الأكاديمية**

> A grand university reading room at night, seen down its length. Long rows of
> wooden study desks recede into the distance, each with its own small lamp lit
> in a warm pool of light. Tall arched windows line the walls and a vaulted
> ceiling disappears into shadow above. Dark cinematic 3D illustration on a
> deep navy-black background, lit only by glowing cyan and violet light with
> soft volumetric haze and shallow depth of field. Subject centred, calm
> composition. Wide cinematic banner. Absolutely no text anywhere in the image
> — no words, letters, numbers, titles, captions, labels, legends, interface
> panels, buttons, logos or watermarks. Nothing written at all. Communicate
> only through shape, light and colour.

## When they're ready

Put them in `Expertise photos/` and say so. `tools/_photos.py` already does the
crop, the compression and the `main.js` sync, so this needs only a thin
`prepare_expertise_photos.py` beside the other two — then the photo layers over
the existing SVG on both the card and the expertise page, in both languages.

Any area still without a photo keeps its drawing, so the grid is never
half-finished. Sources stay out of git, as with the other two sets.
